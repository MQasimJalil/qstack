# Second Opinion

## Requirements

- An independent worker or model authorized to receive the selected inputs.

## Procedure

1. Resolve scope to review of a pinned change, challenge to a named plan, or consultation about a question. Specify evidence and expected output.
2. Select an authorized independent worker and send only the selected inputs. Use read-only access for review and challenge and require evidence or uncertainty on material claims.
3. Check the load-bearing claims against the original scope. Present the outside answer and one specific synthesis recommendation.
