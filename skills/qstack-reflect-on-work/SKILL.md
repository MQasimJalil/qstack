# Reflect On Work

## Procedure

1. Use only the active transcript or a compact digest of it. Gather concrete repeated failures, corrections, and successful methods with evidence from this task.
2. Review the material through judgment, tooling, and divergent lenses. Use separate reviewers where authorized and available or take distinct sequential passes.
3. Validate claims, merge duplicates, and group findings into accepted changes, rejected ideas with reasons, and follow-up work. Prefer a check or other enforcement for a lesson that can be made mechanical.
4. Show each proposed skill destination and change. Apply changes within the user's authorized scope, validate touched skills and links, and report what was applied or remains open.
