# Restate Intent

## Procedure

1. Read the request and its clarifications. Identify the intended outcome, underlying problem, constraints, and open choices.
2. Restate the goal and problem in your own words. Make each explicit assumption visible and distinguish it from a user requirement.
3. Keep the response focused on your understanding. If a material fork remains, state the two readings plainly.
