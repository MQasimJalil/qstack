# Design Alternatives

## Procedure

1. Name the decision and what observation would settle it. Pick two or three alternatives with a material difference in interaction, ownership, or architecture.
2. Build small comparable prototypes with shared data and constraints. Use one visual switcher for an interaction when it makes comparison easier.
3. Drive the same realistic scenarios in each prototype and record the decisive difference. Choose a shape from the evidence and keep the throwaway comparison separate from the implementation.
