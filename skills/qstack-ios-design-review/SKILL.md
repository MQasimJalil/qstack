# iOS Design Review

## Requirements

- An authorized physical iOS device and a screenshot or accessibility interface.

## Procedure

1. Identify the physical device, build and screen list. Open a read-only observation session and capture screenshots and accessibility elements.
2. Assess typography and Dynamic Type, rhythm, contrast, safe areas, hit targets, native behavior, and meaningful states. Tie findings to specific observed elements.
3. Rank findings by user impact, explain what would resolve each, and record screen coverage and device limitations.
