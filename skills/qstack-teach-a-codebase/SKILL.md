# Teach A Codebase

## Procedure

1. Identify what the reader needs to understand to act. Gather the current architecture and, where it matters, earlier design decisions from the record.
2. Define the concept in everyday words and tie it to one concrete example from the project. Walk through the normal flow before adding a relevant constraint or exception.
3. Use the smallest helpful diagram or demo when relationships are easier to see. Add one moving part at a time for complex systems and preserve uncertainty about design rationale.
4. Give the explanation directly at the reader’s depth. Keep source links near technical claims and let the reader choose where to go deeper.
