# Plain Language

## Procedure

1. Read the immediately preceding answer and identify its essential claim and any limitation that changes what the user should infer.
2. Rewrite it in short everyday sentences. Expand an essential technical term the first time it appears or replace it with its concrete effect.
3. Keep the original evidence and uncertainty intact. Stop when the same meaning is clear.
